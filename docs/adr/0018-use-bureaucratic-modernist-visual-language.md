# Use a bureaucratic-modernist visual language

Daylight Saviour will combine premium editorial composition with restrained Australian bureaucratic cues. Oversized time and status typography, a disciplined grid, subtle document marks, and dry status stamps will provide character without a mascot or novelty-app finish. The app icon is the app's mark, and the screens carry its warm gold and its jumped ray so the app and its icon belong together.

## Consequences

- Light presentation uses Civic White `#F7F8FA` page ground, white record surfaces, deep navy ink, slate secondary ink, and cool-neutral rules. Dark presentation remains midnight navy.
- Light Solar Gold is semantic, not decorative: accessible deep gold `#9A6700` marks compact active Time-Zone Data Pack references and Change direction text; muted structural gold marks the utility-header divider and Change Event top rule and left rail. Cool decorative rules remain `#AAB2BC`; neutral control boundaries use the separate contrast-safe `#87919D` token. Signal red is reserved for fictional phase stamps. Settings, Home Time Zone controls, and unrelated rules remain neutral.
- Typography and spacing establish hierarchy before decoration; critical facts remain immediately scannable.
- Forward and Backward Changes may temporarily distort, skip, reverse, or echo grid and type while reduced-motion mode preserves the same meaning without kinetic effects.
- Official-document motifs remain subtle and fictional; the interface must not imply government affiliation. Solar Gold must not become a metallic effect, gradient, or government seal.
- Cartoon clocks, generic gradient-led styling, flags, kangaroos, and other obvious Australian clichés are excluded.
- The app icon is a geometric sun that is also a clock face: warm gold `#FFB238` rays, with the ray at 2 o'clock in signal coral `#FF6A4D` jumped outwards, on midnight navy `#081426`. iOS dark appearance uses dark navy `#05080E`. The mark appears on the app icon, the Android notification icon and the splash screen, which uses midnight navy in both themes. Screens use no other sun or clock illustration.
- Warm gold `#FFB238` may mark structure and text in dark presentation, where it reaches 10.3:1 on midnight navy. In light presentation it reaches only 1.7:1 on Civic White, so it may fill decorative shapes but never text or marks that carry meaning.
- The jumped ray, a signal coral element moved outwards out of sequence, is the visual sign of a Forward Change. Like every colour cue, it never carries meaning alone.
- Final tokens, components, and motion recipes must work in both OS-controlled themes and pass accessibility release gates.
